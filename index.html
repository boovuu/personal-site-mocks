01 / Perspective

A website makes the decisions behind it visible.

A brief may be clear, yet the work can still drift between strategy, content, engineering and the people responsible for each. A website makes that distance visible.

I pay attention to what sits between the parts: the decision that never reached implementation, the measurement no one quite trusts, the publishing system people work around. Good work needs clarity and continuity after launch.

03 / Working principles

Direction. Judgement. Continuity.

Clarity of purpose.

Name the decision before choosing the tool. What should someone understand, what should change, and what can the team maintain?

Judgement in context.

Evidence matters, but so do constraints. A decision has to remain useful after the campaign ends.

Room to grow.

Good systems make the next necessary change easier. They leave room for learning without asking everyone to start again.

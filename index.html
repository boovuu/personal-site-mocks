01 / Perspective

From marketing priorities to a working website.

I work with marketing leaders to decide what a visitor needs to understand and which decision the site should help them make. That becomes a practical brief for the people writing, designing and building it.

I carry that brief through implementation: the pages, the publishing structure, the forms and the measurement. I work across the team to resolve what is missing, check the release and keep the site moving as campaigns and priorities change.

02 / Selected client work

What that means in practice.

Website ownership, audience experiments, discovery and hybrid publishing. Four examples of the work I take on within client and agency teams.

Enterprise browser

01

Taking responsibility for the website between launches

Within an agency team, I own ongoing Webflow delivery for an enterprise browser business: launches and campaign pages, reusable content systems, HubSpot forms, measurement and consent. I work across marketing, design, operations and security to carry changes through to production and keep the site working afterwards.

WOS grew out of that responsibility. I built a workspace for publishing, integrations, indexing and the checks after launch, connecting Webflow content with traffic, search, behaviour and hiring systems. It is the most substantial example of how I turn recurring website work into a system the team can use.

Alongside delivery, I am developing an account-based marketing workflow that connects landing pages, visitor context, relevant content offers and sales follow-up.

Scope and evidenceOngoing website delivery and WOS implementation within a wider team. The detailed case distinguishes implemented integrations from prototype work. ABM remains a developing workflow and demonstration.

Contract management SaaS

02

Learning how different audiences respond

I took three website rebrands through to launch while keeping analytics, SEO and experimentation part of the ongoing work. I built regional demand-generation pages, ran A/B tests and personalisation with Mutiny and VWO, and used Plausible for website analytics.

The recurring question was how different audiences responded: which message helped them understand the offer, which page supported their next decision, and what we should test next. My role covered the implementation, measurement and website work through each launch.

Scope and evidenceThree production rebrands, regional pages, analytics, SEO and experimentation. The example describes delivery responsibilities and the question behind the tests, without claiming a verified conversion lift for an individual experiment.

Tax software

03

Testing how buyers find the product

I test focused landing pages, programmatic pages built around recurring tax questions, and different approaches to answer engine optimisation (AEO). Each gives us a way to explore how a buyer discovers the product and understands where it fits.

The work spans the message on a landing page, the structure of a page family, and the direct answers, FAQs and structured data that make the content easier to interpret. I turn those approaches into working pages that we can inspect, compare and refine.

Scope and evidenceThe author’s account identifies experimentation across landing pages, programmatic content and AEO as the focus. Project records document page templates, direct-answer content and structured data. No conversion, ranking or AI-citation lift is claimed.

Furnished rentals · hybrid platform

04

Sanity + Webflow, working as one website

I work on the marketing and content layer of a hybrid Sanity + Webflow platform, alongside the application team. Vercel document rewrites serve selected Webflow pages through the main site’s URLs, alongside the React and Sanity experience.

The content work spans hundreds of CMS records and two languages. I build and check the templates, Sanity-backed apartment components, city and neighbourhood relationships, links and images that keep those pages coherent. Migration audits and release checks carry that work across the platform boundary.

Scope and evidenceShared platform architecture; my contribution covers Webflow implementation, content and CMS work, Sanity-backed components and verification. Document rewrite describes how pages are served under the main domain.

03 / What I take responsibility for

The brief, the release and what follows.

A brief people can act on.

I help clarify the audience, the decision and the scope. The team can see what needs to change, who needs to contribute and how we will judge the work.

A release that has been checked.

I build in Webflow, connect the CMS, forms and measurement, and check the complete journey before release. I bring unresolved content and specialist decisions back to their owners.

A site the team can keep using.

I maintain templates and integrations, document the work and implement experiments. The next campaign starts with a working system and the context from the last one.

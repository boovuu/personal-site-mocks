The seed is drawn at random. The reading and result are left to you.

A seed ID recreates the same object, light, limit, and medium. Append ?seed=ID to return to a specific draw; on a hosted HTTPS page, each draw updates the URL. The reading and result stay yours to make.

Seed. Reading. Result.

The seed is a starting condition. The reading is the maker’s interpretation. The result is the work that follows. The distinction leaves room for judgement: a prompt does not determine what a piece means.

This page carries the working seed generator from the identity study. A seed ID repeats the same draw. Readings and results are left empty until someone makes them; this is not yet a published logbook or a port of the original engine.

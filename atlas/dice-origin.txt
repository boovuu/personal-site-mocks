D.I.C.E. | The first experiment
Source: Creative AI Prompting Frameworks, a Gemini conversation.
Page numbers refer to the 61-page PDF. Quotation marks preserve the
speaker’s wording, including typing errors. Other text is a summary.

THE OPENING QUESTION — pp. 1–3
Bogdan: “Do you know some creative processes for AI to commit creative output. Basic ai is not creative enough.”
Gemini offered five techniques, including unusual constraints and critique
from different perspectives. Bogdan proposed a hidden tarot reading:
the three-card draw or Celtic Cross could shape an answer, and he wanted
something the user could do that resembled shuffling. Gemini suggested
a four-card layout as another option and a string of characters or five
words from the room as the user's part in the shuffle.

FIRST TRIALS — pp. 5–11
Bogdan tried a branding brief for Bloom, then prompts for game concepts.
For the image test he wanted a prompt to paste into a fresh chat.
Bogdan: “i want to create an image, visually striking, out of this world, never seen before, introducing new color, just beautiful”
His five words were “blessing Wolf roof katarina vera”.

THE SAME REQUEST, TWICE — pp. 11–14
Gemini used Wolf as the animal, roof as the setting, and Katarina as
the woman facing it. It described a new colour made of violet and teal.
Bogdan objected that the words were too obviously combined. Gemini
proposed translating nouns into qualities (wolf into nocturnal movement,
roof into height) and asked for new words.
Bogdan: “No try the same prompt but with new system”
On the retry Gemini kept the woman and wolf and added streams of energy
between them. Bogdan then clarified what he wanted the words to do.
Bogdan: “Words given sre not clues and not subtle, it should be used to throw in the mix to give some other output, completely irrelevant”
Second result: with the original brief and the same five words, Gemini
wrote an image prompt for a white stone sanctuary with a crystalline
fissure and a liquid-metal substance. It described using properties of
the letters instead of the meanings of the words. The transcript shows
the changed output; it does not verify the claimed shuffle algorithm.

A TEST THAT STILL FAILED — pp. 17–19
One of the words for a game concept was “sand”. The game used a shifting
sand bed. Bogdan asked: “why did you use sand?” The older habit persisted.

MAKING IT PORTABLE — pp. 19–24
Bogdan asked for instructions he could paste into a document and later
requested a fresh export to try with another model. The exported rules
still allowed sensory properties of words to influence the result.
These were experiments on the way to D.I.C.E., not its final specification.

THE FIVE OPENING PROPOSALS — pp. 1–2
Gemini named Oblique Strategies prompting, Multi-Perspective Critique
(the Pixar Braintrust Model), the Re-Classification Matrix, Recursive
Looping (the Telephone Method), and Semantic Deconstruction. The Atlas
expands each suggestion with an explanation of what a reader could
inspect in the resulting draft.

AFTER WHITE STONE — pp. 14–24
The homepage, advice, game and orchid trials used fresh sets of five words.
The homepage used “pentagon, viscose, pellets, broom, saps” (pp. 14–16).
The game used “sand, markpliers, forklift, zion, poof” (pp. 17–19).
The orchid image used “komp, ekran, telefon, mis, tastatura” (pp. 21–22).
The export on pp. 23–24 retains the instruction to extract properties
from the words. The stricter rule belongs to the later D.I.C.E. SPEC,
section 1, Law of the Invisible Spice, and section 2 on the draw.

HOW THE COIN ENTERS THE RECORD — later project documents
The founding reading (READING-part1.md, “The division of the trade” and
“Canon flags”) assigns the giving-hand coin to Killick, the astrolabe to
Metis and the five-dot mark to Bogdan. The later design study The Reckoning
Coin, sections “Concept” and “The family”, presents a proposed two-sided
object. The study identifies Cassiopeia on Seed and a 022.5° heading on
Bearing. This connection is documented in the design study. The Gemini
image experiment ends with a prompt for a white stone scene.

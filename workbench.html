The Workbench

The brain of the site, made visible on purpose. Every layer that decides what the site says and looks like, with its file, its why, and its state. The public site shows the work; this page shows the discipline that produces it. It is itself an exhibit: same laws, same tokens, zero decoration.

The stack, back to front

Six layers. Each one can only be changed at its own level; everything downstream inherits.

Layer 1 · canon
Identity & voice law
site-soul.md · sell-by-camouflage.md
How every sentence is written and what selling is allowed to look like: voice mix, spine story, banned list; and the seven sourced rules for hiding intent (Cialdini, Sandler, Greene, Klaff, Enns, Bettger, fair chase).
Layer 2 · truth
Content database
content.duckdb · render-json.sql · build-from-duckdb.js
Cases, principles, exhibits as rows with disclosure and attribution. The drift check fails the build when the site and the database disagree; cases without a disclosure level cannot publish.
Layer 3 · system
Design tokens
tokens.json · token-exceptions.json · scripts/check-tokens.js
Seventeen tokens, each carrying a why. Exceptions exist only with a filed reason and review date. The lint runs in CI on every push.
Layer 4 · copy
Single source of text
site-content.js
Every rendered word. Hero, approach, work, principles, contact, footer, exhibits, plus the internal strategy notes. Nothing is hardcoded in markup.
Layer 5 · surface
The page itself
v9-carries-the-work.html (+ v1–v8 studies, retained)
Skin over the stack: binds SITE, honors tokens, carries the reason toggle. Studies stay online as decision history, not options.
Layer 6 · proof
Identity explorations
identity-studies/reckoning-coin-cassiopeia.html
GPT + Claude study: coin, compass, Cassiopeia; DICE anatomy Seed → Reading → Result. Filed as candidate material for v10, adopted only through the gates below.

The laws

Short list. The full text lives in the canon files; these are the ones that decide arguments.

LAW 1
Check first, define the change, verify it

Nothing ships on a claim. Bindings resolve, lint passes, live URL greps clean. Done means walked.

LAW 2
Copy documents, never argues

Camouflage: if deleting a line changes trust, it stays; if it changes the offer, it goes. Self-awards die on sight.

LAW 3
Every token carries a why

No color, spacing, or weight exists without a stated reason. Exceptions need a filed justification and a review date.

LAW 4
Anonymize by rule, disclose by field

Client identities are masked at the database layer; every case carries disclosure and attribution metadata. What stays quiet stays quiet by design.

LAW 5
Engine room is visible only on purpose

Build logs, drift checks, proofs: documented here, not on the public site. The site shows craft, not plumbing.

LAW 6
One idea per surface

The homepage sells one thesis. Studies and explorations live beside it, clearly labeled, never blended in.

THE RECKONING COIN — CASSIOPEIA IDENTITY STUDY

Open reckoning-coin-cassiopeia.html in a browser. It is a standalone page; no build step is required. Google Fonts load when a connection is available, with local serif fallbacks otherwise.

This version uses Cassiopeia as the coin’s navigational constellation and DICE mark. It connects the coin’s value, two faces, and random toss with a chosen compass bearing and a larger cosmic frame. It preserves the separate Killick, Metis, and Captain’s Seed canon; includes linked historical and astronomy references; leads the personal site with long-term in-house ownership and keeps scoped engagements secondary; and gives DICE stable seed IDs that can be reproduced from the URL.

Homepage project rows and DICE logbook items are illustrative structures, not published case-study claims.

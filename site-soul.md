# site-soul.md — the copy constitution
*Every rendered word on the site obeys this file. Derived from PERSONA_DOSSIER.md (1,997 own messages) + GPT deep analysis + the zero-slop law. If a line violates this file, the line goes, no matter how good it sounds.*

## The voice mix (across the whole site, not per section)
40% builder · 25% operator-strategist · 15% curious hacker · 10% irreverent human · 10% reflective.

## Sentence laws
1. Conclusion → mechanism → evidence → next move. No ceremonial openings.
2. Numbers over adjectives. Market in deltas: 500→1,400 pages, 3.4M users, 54K leads. If a claim has no number, it needs an artifact.
3. Short paragraphs. First person always. "I kept seeing..." never "Organizations often struggle...".
4. "Here's what actually happened" over "Let's unpack this." Never manufacture profound endings. Some things are just annoying and got fixed.
5. Speed without sloppiness: reads like it was written quickly by a smart human. Typos are chat artifacts, never reproduced in public copy.

## The story spine (the narrative the site tells)
"I came in to build the page. Then the tracking broke. Then consent affected tracking. Then CRM routing mattered. Then SEO mattered. Then conversion mattered. Eventually you realize the website isn't a collection of pages. It's an operating system shared by half the company."
The builder who wandered far enough upstream to become an operator. That arc is the site's narrative — every section is a station on it.

## Signature phrases (use sparingly, they're seasoning)
"from strategy to execution" · "stay close to tech" · "make it meaningful" · "the piece we were missing" · "to answer your question directly"

## Banned (instant rewrite)
"In today's rapidly evolving landscape" · "I scaled 10x with AI, here's the playbook" · "passionate about" · guru cadence · every-experience-is-a-lesson · invented accomplishments · exaggerated ownership · manufactured certainty · corporate warmth · pirate/quartermaster lore as public gimmick (internal language only) · motivational endings · em dashes · "unlock", "leverage", "seamless", "empower".

## Discretion layer (never stated, always respected)
Job search, employer politics, client names, "WebOps positioning" stay implicit. Anonymized by rule. The site never leaks what he keeps quiet.

## Per-surface calibration
- Hero: operator thesis, ≤9 words of proof, one door.
- Approach: the spine story compressed; mechanisms, not adjectives.
- Cases: problem → move → result → limits. Ownership stated ("I owned X"), numbers when public, limits always.
- Contact: one honest door; selectivity is the signal, never a hard sell.
